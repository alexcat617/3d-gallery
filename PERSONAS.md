# Personas

Two roles. Every FEAT file tags stories and acceptance criteria with `P1` / `P2`.

## P1: Alex, the artist (sets up the pitch)

| | |
|---|---|
| Context | Local artist approaching two restaurants about showing work. Also building this app, for the first time. |
| Devices | Windows laptop (integrated graphics) + phone for photos and measuring. |
| Wants | Walk in (or send a link) with something that looks professional and answers "what would it look like here?" |
| Struggles | Describing and selling own work; knowing who to talk to; feeling amateur showing phone photos. |
| Needs from us | A clear capture checklist (what to measure, what to photograph); add artwork with just a photo + size; arrange pieces by tapping/dragging, no code; preview exactly what the owner will see. |

## P2: Dana, the venue stakeholder (views the pitch)

| | |
|---|---|
| Context | Restaurant owner or manager. Busy, on their feet, often between services. Not interested in learning an app. |
| Devices | Mostly phone (one hand, sometimes in the dining room itself); sometimes a laptop in the office. |
| Time | 1–3 minutes. |
| Wants | To quickly judge: does this art fit my space, my vibe, my walls? How big is it? |
| Fears | Clunky tech, tiny controls, wasting time, committing to something that looks wrong. |
| Needs from us | Opens instantly from a link; obvious "tap to see the next wall"; real sizes stated plainly; nothing to install, no sign-in. |

## Future: P3, other artists

When the app scales: artists without technical skills setting up their own pitches. Keep P1 tasks simple enough that P3 could do them later.
